Mainframe Analytics: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Who uses this app

Mainframe Analytics is an internal tool used only by Mainframe Media (contact: mainframemediahq@gmail.com) to run the YouTube channels and Facebook Pages it owns: Redacted Hour, In the Margins, Main Street Math, The Hidden Machinery and Sudo Architect. It is not offered to the public, and no one outside Mainframe Media can sign in to it.

What the app does

  • Statistics (read-only): reads aggregated statistics for the company's own channels and Pages to write weekly performance reports.
  • Publishing: uploads and schedules videos, Shorts and Reels that Mainframe Media made to its own YouTube channels and Facebook Pages, with their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions.
  • Comments: reads comments on the company's own videos and posts so that Mainframe Media can reply to them, and hides or removes spam and abusive comments. Every reply is approved by a person at Mainframe Media before it is sent.

What it accesses

  • YouTube: channel details, the list of uploaded videos, and aggregated statistics such as views, watch time, impressions, click-through rate, audience retention, traffic sources and subscriber counts; uploading videos and managing the channel's videos, playlists, captions and comments.
  • Facebook: Page follower counts and aggregated Page and video insights; publishing videos and Reels to the Page; reading, replying to and hiding comments on the Page's posts.
  • Comment data: for comments on the company's own videos and posts, the commenter's public display name, the comment text and its time, exactly as the platform shows them publicly.

The app does not access passwords, payment details, private messages, email addresses, friend lists or any other data from viewers' own accounts.

How the data is used and stored

  • Statistics are used only for performance reports on the company's own channels.
  • Comments are used only to read and answer them on the same video or post, and to understand what viewers ask for in future videos.
  • Access tokens, reports and comment records are stored on the company's own computer. Data is never sold, never shared with or disclosed to third parties, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models.
  • Comment records are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted. Reports keep only aggregated numbers.

Use of YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, channel owners agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google account permissions page. Facebook access can be removed in Facebook's Settings > Business integrations. When access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and Mainframe Media deletes them.

Data deletion

To have data about you deleted (for example the record of a comment you left on one of our videos), email mainframemediahq@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion", the platform (YouTube or Facebook) and the name you comment under. We delete the matching records within 30 days and confirm by email. Deleting your comment on YouTube or Facebook also removes it from anything the app reads later.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this same address with a new date.
